# Admin Community Management Guide

## Overview

Admins have comprehensive tools to manage the community system through the **Admin Dashboard** → **Community Management** section.

## Access Community Management

1. **Login as Admin** at `/admin`
2. **Click "Community Management"** card (shows new posts count)
3. **Or go directly** to `/admin/community`

---

## 🎛️ Admin Community Dashboard

### **4 Main Tabs:**

### 1. **📊 Overview Tab**
- **Community Stats**: Members, Posts, Groups, Active Discussions
- **Recent Activity**: Latest forum posts with engagement metrics
- **Quick Insights**: Real-time community health metrics

### 2. **💬 Forum Posts Tab**
- **View All Posts**: Complete list with details
- **Moderation Actions**:
  - ✅ **Publish/Archive** posts
  - 🗑️ **Delete** inappropriate content
  - 📊 **View Stats**: Replies, likes, views
- **Post Details**: Title, Author, Category, Status, Date
- **Bulk Actions**: Refresh, filter, search

### 3. **🏘️ Groups Tab**
- **Community Groups**: All wellness groups
- **Group Details**: Name, members, description, category
- **Group Management**: View membership, activity
- **Group Categories**: Wellness, Ayurveda, Mental Health, etc.

### 4. **📂 Categories Tab**
- **Forum Categories**: Wellness, Ayurveda, Mental Health, Success Stories
- **Category Settings**: Icons, colors, descriptions, order
- **Status Management**: Active/Inactive categories
